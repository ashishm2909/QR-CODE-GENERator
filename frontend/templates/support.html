How do I download my QR code?

Once generated, simply click the "Download SVG" or "Download PNG" button below the QR preview.

Can I change the destination of my QR code?

Yes, if you use our Dynamic QR features (available on Pro and Business plans). Standard QR codes are static and cannot be changed once printed.
